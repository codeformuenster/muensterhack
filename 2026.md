## Leezenpass

- A digital bike pass for Münster that makes stolen bikes hard to sell: register your bike in 60 seconds with a photo (AI detects type, colours, brand and frame number), check a frame number before buying a used bike, prove ownership with your receipt and a one-time code, and report thefts. Goodwill points reward confirmed help from the community and strengthen solidarity among cyclists.
- **Team:** Adarsh, Frank, Niharika, Philipp, Viola
- **Code:** [MunsterHack-LeezenPass](https://github.com/iamAdarshh/MunsterHack-LeezenPass)
